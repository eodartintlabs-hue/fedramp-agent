### 3.1 `AFC` — Addressing FedRAMP Communication

- **Status:** `stable` (`FRR.AFC.info.status`)
- **Effective Dates:** All: obtain `2026-01-05`, maintain `2026-01-05`, grace `2026-07-01`
- **Purpose:** The Addressing FedRAMP Communication rules (formerly FedRAMP Security Inbox) ensure FedRAMP can reliably contact the security and compliance staff responsible for every FedRAMP-authorized cloud service offering. These rules also set expectations for urgent communications, response time testing, and routing important messages separately from general support or customer service channels.
- **Excluded Non-Provider Subsets in `AFC`** *(detailed in Section 4)*: `AFC.all.FRP` (FedRAMP Responsibilities, affects `FedRAMP`, 8 rules)

> **Normative Interpretation Rules for KSIs (`fedramp-cr26-mrf/AGENTS.md`):**
> 1. **NO RFC-2119 Force:** KSI indicators and their `varies_by_class` variants carry **NO `force` field**. Never invent `MUST`/`SHOULD` keywords for a KSI; instead, evaluate KSIs through their capability statement alongside binding 20x process rules (`FRC-CSX-MAS`, `FRC-CSX-VVK` [Class C >=2 automated V&V methods; Class D >=4 automated V&V methods], `SDR-CSX-KSI`, `SDR-CSX-KMT`, `IVV-CSX-AIA`).

> 3. **Default KSI Artifacts (`info.default_artifacts.KSI`) & `FRD-DTM`:** Every KSI expects 5 default artifacts: (i) explanation of measures/objectives (or reason & customer risk if unavailable), (ii) persistence cycle explanation, (iii) verification that measures demonstrate the KSI, (iv) verification that automation is accurate and sufficient, and (v) validation that measures are accurately produced and working as intended. Per **`FRD-DTM`**, probabilistic generative AI outputs MUST NOT be used to generate deterministic telemetry for KSI validation.
